# QuestMobAutoMarker

**QuestMobAutoMarker** is a lightweight and efficient World of Warcraft addon designed to automatically assign raid target markers (like a skull or cross) to quest-objective mobs as soon as you target, hover over them, or encounter them in the game world. Never lose track of your specific quest targets in a crowded pack of monsters again!

---

### ✨ Key Features

* **Smart Quest Auto-Detection:** Automatically scans your active quest objectives (using Questie integration or built-in tooltip parsing) to identify exact target requirements.
* **Automatic Targeting Markers:** Instantly marks qualifying mobs with a rotating sequence of raid icons to help you easily distinguish them.
* **Boss & Rare Scanner:** Includes a customizable background scanner to automatically flag specific rare or boss targets the moment they appear on your nameplates, target, focus, or mouseover.
* **Minimap Button Integration:** Features a clean, movable minimap icon (powered by LibDBIcon) that lets you quickly toggle options or enable/disable the addon on the fly.
* **Flexible Customization:** A clean configuration interface (`/automark` or `/am`) allowing you to choose which raid icons to use, toggle combat restrictions, or disable the addon in instances/groups.

---

### 🎮 How to Use

1. Install the addon into your `Interface/AddOns/` folder.
2. In-game, type **/automark** or **/am** to open the options panel and customize your preferred markers.
3. Use the **Minimap Button** to quickly open the configuration menu or right-click it to toggle the addon on/off. 

### 📋 Requirements
* **Questie** (Optional, but recommended for the most seamless quest objective matching).
